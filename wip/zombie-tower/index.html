Zombie Tower

Status: Playable, early build

Fight your way up 50 floors of the biotech tower where the outbreak started. Clear every zombie on a floor to open the stairs, pick a new gun, armor piece or weapon mod between floors, and take on a boss every 10 floors.

Play Zombie Tower

Before you play

  • Phone: turn it sideways. Drag on the left half to move; your character aims and shoots on his own. Buttons on the right: shove, reload, swap guns.
  • Computer: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold left click to shoot. Space to shove, R to reload, Q to swap guns, Esc to pause.
  • Your progress saves in this browser. Dying sends you back to your last checkpoint (after each boss, and halfway through each zone).
  • Still being built: difficulty is still being tuned, and the guns and backgrounds are temporary art.
