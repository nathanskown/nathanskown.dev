How each schedule works

Sources

S&P 500, 400 and 600, quarterly rebalance. Changes take effect before the open on the Monday after the third Friday of March, June, September and December, and are usually announced about two weeks earlier, after the close on the first Friday of the month (September 2026: announced Sept. 4, effective Sept. 21). Additions also happen off-cycle when a member is acquired. S&P DJI

Russell US indexes, now twice a year. From 2026 FTSE Russell reconstitutes in June and December. December 2026: rank day Oct. 30, preliminary lists Nov. 13, lockdown from Nov. 30, effective after the close Dec. 11. The June reshuffle has historically been one of the heaviest trading days of the year. FTSE Russell

Nasdaq-100, annual. Reconstituted each December, effective before the open on the Monday after the third Friday; announced about a week earlier (2025: announced Dec. 12, effective Dec. 22). Nasdaq

MSCI index reviews. Quarterly, with dates set in advance by MSCI. MSCI

13F deadline. Managers with over $100 million in US stocks must file holdings within 45 days of each quarter end (moved to the next business day when it falls on a weekend). This site rebuilds its ownership data after each deadline.

Quarterly options expiration. The third Friday of March, June, September and December, when stock options, index options and futures expire together; S&P and Nasdaq-100 changes are timed to it.
