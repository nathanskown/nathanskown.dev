{{reactionNote}}

Each move is measured from the price at 2:00 p.m. ET, when the statement came out. “At the close” is the last reading up to 4:00 p.m. ET; “Whole day” is the usual close-to-close change, so it includes the hours before the statement. The S&P 500 and the dollar index are percent changes, yields are basis points (100 bp = 1 point). The 5- and 30-minute 2-year moves are approximate: Yahoo has no intraday 2-year yield, so they are implied from 2-year Treasury note futures (about 1.85 years of duration). The whole-day 2-year move is the official Treasury par yield. Five-minute prices are only kept for about 60 days, so older meetings show the whole-day move only.
